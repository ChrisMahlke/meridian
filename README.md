<h1 align="center">MERIDIAN SUPPORT</h1>

<p align="center"><sub>A MINIMAL INSTRUMENT<br>FOR DIRECTION, THE SUN, THE MOON, AND THE TIME</sub></p>

Meridian puts the sun, the moon, your heading, and the time on one quiet dial, for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. This repository is where to report a problem, suggest something, or ask a question. It holds no source code.

## Before you open an issue

The [Support page](https://chrismahlke.github.io/meridian/support/) answers common questions, from reading the dial to moving through time, and lists what Meridian needs.

## Open an issue

- [Report a problem](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/meridian-support/issues/new?template=report-a-problem.yml)
- [Suggest something](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/meridian-support/issues/new?template=suggest-something.yml)
- [Ask a question](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/meridian-support/issues/new?template=ask-a-question.yml)

You'll need a free GitHub account. Or email [cmahlke@gmail.com](mailto:cmahlke@gmail.com). I read everything.

For a problem, it helps to include your device, its iOS or watchOS version, the nearest town, the time Meridian showed, and what you expected to see. Issues are public, so leave out anything you'd rather not share, such as your exact location, or send it by email.
